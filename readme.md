Presenting the tga.zone complete collection! What once started as a portfolio piece, I meandered around and got bogged down with other projects. Eventually, it became what you see today, a memorial to my interests, hubris, and failure.

Interests, well, look at it! Who needs this? E3 bingo was a cute joke when the century was young. Angry Gamer's 2006 bingo card was the first mention I could find (lost to time, naturally). Why make a living site with as many bells and whistles as I'm capable of creating? Cuz like many long roads, I thought it would be shorter. Or more focused. Or more of a year-round thing than a thing I work on for 50 hours in the two weeks leading up to the event.

And there's my hubris, where I figured I was capable of completing a project with this many new skills to learn in any short amount of time. I was a ginger novice to web application development when I started this in 2018. Or 2020. Or 2022. Or 2024. Usually so much time passed that the entire web development world starts moving onto some new hot framework when I decide to focus on it again. Except you express, you workhorse, you.

Sadly, this is also my failure to adapt. I lost my job in 2023 with years of experience, happy co-workers, and a popular language on my resume. Work in software engineering would elude me for the next 36 months. Eventually I looked back employment gap--and forward to the road ahead--with reborn eyes, and accepted the truth. Thousands of resumes, dozens of interviews, sleepless nights, teary-eyed days, to realize that I had become another demographically average societal dropout. And AI would only make it even more difficult to find work in the coming years.

So, as I continue to plod on this (something that I know is not nearly polished, presentable, or featured enough to stand next to _real_ web apps) I attend school. I relearn to swallow my pride, which apparently has been a very difficult thing for me to do. But as of this summer of 2026, I see the future of writing code by hand the same as hand-sewing your own everyday clothing, or aspiring to own a horse drawn carridge as primary transportation. Novel, but unnecessary.

So visit tga.zone! Participate in a worse version of toys that other people have built faster than me! And Aaron, so long as I'm oversharing to no one who cares, I might as well add you, my brother, to the mix. I love you, and assure you whatever this is, it'll be a story on the journey. The world will only have my will to live after they've killed me, it's the only way.
